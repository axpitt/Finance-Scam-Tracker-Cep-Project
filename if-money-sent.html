Actionable Incident Guidance

If Money Was Sent: What to Do Next

If you suspect you have transferred funds to a fraudulent investment promoter or fake trading app, maintain composure. Follow these official, time-sensitive steps immediately to minimize losses.

Important Reality: Lodging a complaint with your bank, police, or cybercrime authorities is the official and necessary route, but it does not guarantee financial recovery. Act quickly to maximize the chance of freezing funds before they are withdrawn through mule accounts.
Step 01

Contact Your Bank or Payment App Immediately

Notify your bank, credit card company, or UPI payment app customer support right away through their official verified phone numbers. Request an urgent freeze on the disputed transaction and ask for a formal transaction dispute acknowledgement / ticket number.

Time Critical: First 2–3 Hours
Step 02 • Emergency

Call 1930 & Lodge Cybercrime Complaint

In India, immediately dial the National Cyber Financial Fraud Helpline at 1930 or register a complaint online at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in). Provide transaction IDs, sender/receiver bank account details, and timings.

Official Helpline: 1930
Step 03

Preserve All Electronic Evidence

Take detailed screenshots of chat conversations, promoter usernames, phone numbers, payment receipts, UPI Reference/UTR numbers, bank statements, and app screens. Save these in a secure folder for law enforcement. Do not post sensitive financial proof publicly on social media.

Evidence Protection
Step 04 • Caution

Beware of "Fund Recovery" Scammers

Anyone on social media, Telegram, or email claiming: "We are ethical hackers / legal recovery experts who can get your money back for a 20% advance fee" is running a secondary recovery scam. Regulated authorities and law enforcement never ask for advance fees to recover money.

Never Pay for Recovery
Step 05

For Regulated Securities Entities: SEBI SCORES

If your complaint involves a genuine SEBI-registered broker, investment advisor, or portfolio manager, file a formal grievance on the official SEBI SCORES Portal (scores.sebi.gov.in) after first approaching the intermediary.
