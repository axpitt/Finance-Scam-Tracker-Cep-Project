Critical Warning Signs

6 Red Flags in Online Investment Promotions

A warning sign is a reason to pause, step back, and verify. While an individual indicator does not prove misconduct on its own, spotting these patterns protects your savings against high-risk fraudulent traps.

01
High Risk Sign

Guaranteed or Unusually High Returns

Any promotion claiming "100% fixed profit," "zero risk," or daily guaranteed returns in stock, options, or crypto trading violates core market realities. Real securities markets fluctuate constantly, and no regulated entity can guarantee future market profits.

What to do:

Pause immediately. Treat guaranteed return promises as a major warning sign. Disregard claims of "insider algorithms" and check SEBI investor advisories.

02
High Risk Sign

Pressure to Act Quickly

Messages featuring countdown timers, phrases like "join before midnight," "limited slots left," or "act today or miss the bull run" are psychological tricks designed to bypass calm scrutiny and prevent you from consulting trusted advisors or researching independently.

What to do:

Never transfer money under time pressure. Legitimate investments do not expire in 15 minutes. Step away from the screen, calm down, and consult official sources.

03
High Risk Sign

Unverified Guru or Private Tip Group

A large social media following, rented luxury backdrops, verified blue checkmarks, or paid Telegram/WhatsApp channels do not prove financial competence, honesty, or regulatory registration. Unregulated tip providers often promote pump-and-dump schemes.

What to do:

Verify registration directly on SEBI's Recognized Intermediaries directory. Remember that even a registered name can be impersonated; contact the entity only via official directory contacts.

04
High Risk Sign

Profit Screenshots as "Proof"

Screenshots showing green profit charts, bank balance notifications, or supposed member testimonials are trivial to fabricate with simple editing tools, inspect-element scripts, or paper-trading demo accounts. They never constitute verified financial performance.

What to do:

Do not treat digital images or video clips as evidence of genuine profits. Ask for audited statutory statements and independent exchange verification.

05
High Risk Sign

Unfamiliar App Link or Direct APK File

Scammers frequently circulate direct download links (`.apk` files or obscure sideload websites) instead of recognized app stores. These apps mimic legitimate trading terminals but are controlled entirely by fraudsters who manipulate the displayed values.

What to do:

Never install apps sent via direct messaging or social links. Regulated Indian brokers and mutual fund distributors must be listed with NSE, BSE, or SEBI.

06
Severe Danger Sign

More Money Required to Withdraw

When a user attempts to withdraw their "profits," the platform abruptly freezes the request and demands 10%–30% in "clearance taxes," "regulatory fees," or "conversion charges." In reality, the profits are completely fictional and any additional money paid is instantly stolen.

What to do:

STOP IMMEDIATELY. Do not transfer another single rupee. Legitimate brokers deduct applicable taxes at source from your balance. Report to 1930 right away.
