Academic Project Details

About This Community Engagement Project

This educational website was conceived and developed as part of the Community Engagement Project (CEP) curriculum at MIT Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar for SY B.Tech students in Academic Year 2026–27.

Curriculum Engagement Mission

Project Scope & Educational Mission

The objective of this Community Engagement Project is to design, test, and deliver clear, accessible, and practical digital investor-awareness materials for youth, college students, and first-time digital market participants.

With the rapid rise of mobile trading and social media promotion, deceptive schemes have proliferated. This project translates official regulatory guidance from SEBI and national cybercrime agencies into plain-language educational modules that empower citizens to evaluate digital investment hype critically.

The project is deliberately structured with zero external dependencies, no user tracking, and in-memory evaluation mechanics to protect student and visitor privacy.

Non-Commercial Educational Declaration: This project is strictly non-commercial and educational. It does not provide financial advice, broker recommendations, investment evaluations, or endorsement of any securities.
