Scam or Smart Deal?
Spot Online Investment Hype.

Unsolicited chat groups, social media ads, and direct messages frequently promise guaranteed daily stock profits or invite you to download exclusive private trading apps. This student-led awareness project equips you to recognize deceptive warning signs, pause before transferring funds, and verify offers through independently located official channels.

Key Reminder: All securities-market investments carry inherent market risk. Past performance, follower counts, and screenshots do not guarantee future returns. No legitimate regulated platform promises guaranteed or risk-free profits.
